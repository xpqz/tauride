# Reap a spawned interpreter's whole process group

Implements [Tauride #150](https://github.com/xpqz/tauride/issues/150). Branch `claude/150-reap-process-group`.

A detached spawn (the local interpreter's `mapl` wrapper, which runs `dyalog`) is tracked by its process group, not only its leader's pid. `proc_kill` signals the group; the group stays recorded until it is empty, and app exit sends SIGTERM and then SIGKILL to every recorded group, so an interpreter whose wrapper has gone is still ended. The connecting dialog's cancel sends SIGKILL, as Dyalog serving RIDE ignores SIGTERM.

## Tests

`test/_tauri.js` passes an `RIDE_CONNECT` option through to the app, so a test can connect Tauride to an interpreter it did not start.

| Test | Pins |
|---|---|
| `test/session-lifecycle.js` `an interpreter whose wrapper was killed is reaped when Tauride quits` (macOS) | SIGTERM to the wrapper alone leaves the interpreter running and Tauride reports it; after dismissing that report, Cmd-Q ends the interpreter and Tauride exits with status 0. |
| `test/session-lifecycle.js` `killing a detached spawn ends its whole process group` | Through the real shim, `kill('SIGKILL')` on a detached spawn ends a member of its group that ignores SIGTERM. |
| `test/external-interpreter.js` `quitting Tauride leaves running an interpreter it connected to but did not start` (macOS) | An interpreter started outside Tauride and serving RIDE, to which Tauride connects with `RIDE_CONNECT`, is still running and still serving after Cmd-Q; Tauride exits with status 0. A guard: it passes on `main` and must keep passing. |
| `proc::tests::reap_ends_a_group_whose_leader_has_exited` | A recorded group whose leader has exited is ended at exit, including a member that ignores SIGTERM. |
| `proc::tests::signal_reaches_every_member_of_a_detached_spawn` | A signal to a detached spawn reaches every member of its group; an unknown id is an error. |
| `proc::tests::signal_reaches_only_the_process_of_a_spawn_without_its_own_group` | A spawn without its own group is signalled alone, as before: the other member is still alive 500 ms later. |
| `proc::tests::a_group_is_forgotten_once_it_is_empty` | A group stays recorded while it has a member and is forgotten once empty, so a later process cannot be signalled through a reused id. |

## Design decisions

- Ruled: a recorded group is reaped whole. Anything the spawned interpreter starts without its own process group belongs to that session and ends with Tauride, even if another Tauride has since connected to it. An interpreter started any other way is never recorded, so never signalled (`test/external-interpreter.js`).
- Only groups Tauride created are recorded: a detached spawn is put in its own group, so the group id is the spawn's pid.
- A group stays recorded after its leader exits until it has no members, checked once a second, then is forgotten, so its id is not signalled after the system may have reused it. At exit, a group that cannot be signalled is skipped.
- The existing walk of spawned process trees stays: it covers spawns that are not detached.
- `proc_kill` on a detached spawn signals its group, a departure from Node's `kill`; its one caller is the connecting dialog's cancel, which now sends SIGKILL because Dyalog serving RIDE ignores SIGTERM.
- When the wrapper dies, Tauride still reports `Interpreter received SIGTERM` in a native sheet. macOS defers quitting while the sheet is open; the native test dismisses it before Cmd-Q.
- Windows keeps `taskkill /T` and is unchanged.

### Mutations

Each applied alone to `proc.rs`; every one is caught. The unit-test mutations were rerun after the tests gained a cleanup guard and the 500 ms check.

| Mutation | Fails |
|---|---|
| `signal` targets the process, never the group | `signal_reaches_every_member_of_a_detached_spawn` |
| `signal` targets a group even without one | `signal_reaches_only_the_process_of_a_spawn_without_its_own_group` |
| `reap` ignores recorded groups | `reap_ends_a_group_whose_leader_has_exited` |
| `reap` never escalates to SIGKILL | `reap_ends_a_group_whose_leader_has_exited` |
| a group is forgotten at once | `a_group_is_forgotten_once_it_is_empty` |
| a group is never forgotten | `a_group_is_forgotten_once_it_is_empty` |
| `proc_spawn` records no group (native run) | both native pins in `test/session-lifecycle.js` |

The first `signal` mutation survived until the test waited 500 ms: a member that had just been killed was still a zombie, which `kill(pid, 0)` reports as alive.

## Noted

- The switch of the connecting dialog's cancel to SIGKILL has no pin of its own; the shim test pins that a detached spawn's `kill('SIGKILL')` ends its group.
- At exit, skipping a group that cannot be signalled has no pin: an id reused by another user's group cannot be arranged in a test.
